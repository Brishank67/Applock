AruShield Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026  |  App version 1.0.0

In short: AruShield does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. Your PIN and settings stay on your device only.

संक्षेप में: AruShield आपका कोई व्यक्तिगत डेटा इकट्ठा, साझा या बेचता नहीं है। ऐप में इंटरनेट की अनुमति ही नहीं है, इसलिए कुछ भी आपके फ़ोन से बाहर नहीं जाता। आपका PIN और सेटिंग्स सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में रहती हैं।

AruShield is an Android app that locks other apps on your phone with a PIN or fingerprint. It is developed by AruShield Tech, Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, India. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information the app stores on your device

AruShield keeps the following only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your PIN, saved as a salted one-way hash. The app never stores the PIN itself.
  • Your security question choice and your answer, also saved as a hash.
  • The list of apps you chose to lock (their package names).
  • Your settings, such as auto-lock time, fingerprint on or off, and protection on or off.

This information never leaves your phone. Android backup is turned off for the app, so it is not copied to cloud backups either. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account. The app has no sign-up.
  • No location, contacts, photos, messages or files.
  • No advertising ID, analytics or crash reporting.
  • No third-party SDKs, and no ads.

Permissions and why the app needs them

  • Usage access: to detect which app is currently open, so the lock screen can appear when you open a locked app. The app only compares it with your locked list. It does not record or upload your app usage.
  • Display over other apps: to show the lock screen on top of a locked app.
  • Device administrator (optional): turned on only if you enable Uninstall Protection, so others cannot remove the app without your PIN. You can turn it off in the app's Settings at any time.
  • Foreground service and notification: to keep protection running in the background. A low-priority notification is shown while it runs.
  • Start after reboot: to restart protection automatically after your phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint (biometric): to let you unlock with your fingerprint. Android handles the fingerprint check. AruShield never sees or stores your fingerprint data.
  • Installed apps list: to show the apps you can choose to lock. This list stays on your phone.
  • Vibrate: for feedback on the PIN pad.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell or transfer any information to anyone, because the app does not collect or transmit any.

Forgot your PIN

You can reset your PIN by answering the security question you set. Because nothing is stored outside your phone, we cannot recover or reset a PIN for you. Choose a security answer you will remember.

Children

AruShield does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. The app collects no personal data from any user.

Deleting your data

To remove everything AruShield has stored, uninstall the app. If Uninstall Protection is on, first turn it off in AruShield's Settings, then uninstall.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
